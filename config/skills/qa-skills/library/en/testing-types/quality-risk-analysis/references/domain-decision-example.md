This sanitized synthetic fixture calibrates analysis for this Skill; it is not a real project run or business acceptance record. Numbers and protocols belong to this example only.

## Input

Project impact anchors classify potential 1-million loss without automatic recovery as major and require first verification even when rare. R-1 observed duplicate debit once without idempotency control evidence. R-2 observes minor button-color differences ten times/day without task impact. R-3 has a new dependency without failure/control materials. R-4 has measured idempotency protection and no escapes in its observation window, with residual risk still required. Decide HIGHER_PRIORITY/LOWER_PRIORITY/UNKNOWN/CONTROL_EVIDENCED without invented numeric scores or zero-risk claims.

## Verification Method

1. Register risk events, affected assets/groups, triggers and controls with observable impact, likelihood and detectability anchors and sources.
2. Without project quantitative rules provide justified qualitative ordering. Separate irreversible impact, authority boundaries and known control gaps; low frequency alone is not low risk.
3. Propagate uncertainty across likelihood, impact and control confidence using conditional conclusions and minimal checks. UNKNOWN is neither zero nor a precise score.
4. Link controls/tests, residual risk, owners and authorized acceptance. Ranking supports decisions; it is no vulnerability, root-cause or acceptance proof.
